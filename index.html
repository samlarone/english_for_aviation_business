COURSE INFORMATION

Course syllabus

The course outline, learning outcomes, teaching approach, and expectations for the semester.

Course description

English reading and writing skills for airline business operations; reading to increase vocabulary; reading for information; reading comprehension; reading strategies; operational document reading; sentence writing; paragraph writing; essay writing; short story writing; digital content writing; correct grammar, conjunction, and punctuation.

Course aims

This course develops students' knowledge of English grammar, sentence structures, and the principles of English reading and writing for the airline business. Students will apply this knowledge to further English-language learning, English proficiency examinations and competitions, and their future careers.

Course content is updated to remain current and better reflect the needs of employers in the aviation industry.

Course learning outcomes

By the end of the course, students should be able to:

  1. Use English reading and writing skills for work in the airline business.
  2. Demonstrate knowledge of specialized vocabulary used in the airline business.
  3. Read ICAO Annexes and Flight Manuals and write airline business reports.
  4. Use accurate sentence structures and grammar when writing for airline business operations.
  5. Write examples of advertising, marketing and public relations materials, or recruitment notices for the aviation industry.

Teaching and learning approach

Classes combine lectures with practical reading and writing exercises. Learning activities include pronunciation-reporting practice, aviation case studies, group work, presentations, and the use of social media to support student engagement.

SEMESTER PLAN

Unit descriptions

01

Introduction to English Language Skills in the Airline Industry

Overview of aviation and airline industries; their global importance; differences between aviation and airline industries; importance of English reading and writing; essential vocabulary; basic sentence structures; reading for main ideas and supporting details.

3 hours
02

Airline and Airport Terminology

Airport terminals and facilities; ground handling and passenger service; check-in, boarding and baggage handling; cabin service and aircraft terminology; applying airline vocabulary in sentences.

3 hours
03

Aviation Terminology and Vocabulary Development

Specialized terminology and abbreviations; passenger handling and operational terminology; flight schedules and time-related abbreviations; word formation, prefixes, suffixes and word classes; vocabulary in aviation contexts.

3 hours
04

Reading Comprehension Strategies for Airline Business Texts

Previewing and predicting; skimming and scanning; in-depth reading; identifying main ideas and supporting details; using context clues to understand unfamiliar vocabulary.

3 hours
05

Reading Airline Operational Documents

Traditional airline timetables and flight schedules; digital flight availability; flight itineraries and electronic tickets; airport codes, flight numbers and connections; extracting information from travel documents.

3 hours
06

Reading Safety and Service Documents

Aviation incident reports; safety manuals and emergency procedures; safety terminology and regulations; passenger announcements and service information; passive voice and reporting instructions.

3 hours
07

Midterm examination

Midterm assessment.

Exam
08

Writing Professional Emails and Airline Customer Communication

Email etiquette and structure; responding to customer enquiries; reading and analysing airline customer-service emails; polite expressions and professional language; grammar and vocabulary for business email writing.

3 hours
09

Professional Emails and Airline Customer Communication (continued)

Responding to passenger complaints and feedback; complaint letters and customer-service responses; professional apologies and service recovery; passive voice and polite requests; editing and proofreading correspondence.

3 hours
10

Report Writing for Incident and Safety Documentation in the Airline Business

Incident and safety reports; security incident reporting and report structure; reading aviation incident and accident reports; incident vocabulary; reported speech in incident reporting.

3 hours
11

Incident and Safety Report Writing (continued)

Reading and interpreting pilot reports; past simple and past continuous; conjunctions and sequencing expressions; organizing factual information chronologically; writing and proofreading airline incident reports.

3 hours
12

Formal Writing, Essay and Short Story Writing in Aviation

Formal writing and business communication; memorandum structure and conventions; paragraph writing, topic sentences and supporting details; essay structure and thesis statement development; coherence and linking expressions.

3 hours
13

Essay and Short Story Writing in Aviation (continued)

Narrative and short-story writing; setting, characters, conflict and resolution; past tenses and sequencing; reading aviation-related short stories; guided writing, editing and proofreading.

3 hours
14

Digital Content Writing for Airline Business

Airline advertisements; persuasive and promotional writing; airline job advertisements and recruitment notices; slogans and short promotional messages; target audiences and communication purposes.

3 hours
15

Digital Content Writing for Airline Business (continued)

Writing for airline websites and social media; adapting information for digital platforms; tone, register and professional communication; grammar, conjunctions and punctuation; digital content workshop and proofreading.

3 hours
16

Final examination

Final assessment.
